Experimental, unexpected lighting: colored gels (deep red, teal, magenta) mixed with warm daylight,
hard light through patterned or broken shapes (blinds, lace, leaves, glass, prisms), light leaks and lens flare,
strong shadows across the face, mixed color temperatures. The light should feel bold and surprising,
but the face must stay clearly visible and the skin realistic.
